# Klask Research Notes

The implementation uses a deliberately approximate scale: the real board is represented as a 4.3 by 3.3 unit play field, roughly matching a 43 cm by 33 cm field.

## Rules Used

- First player to 6 points wins.
- Three white magnetic obstacles, commonly called biscuits, start in the center.
- A player scores when the ball lands in the opponent goal and stays there.
- A player scores when two or three biscuits stick to the opponent striker.
- A player scores when the opponent pulls their own striker into their own goal.
- A player scores when the opponent loses control of their striker.
- After a point, biscuits reset to their center marks and the non-scoring player kicks off from a corner.

## Physical Anchors

- The official FAQ lists the board at approximately 14 in by 18 in and 5.5 in tall.
- Retail listings commonly describe the playing field as about 43 cm by 33 cm.
- One product listing describes the goal hole as about 3 cm in diameter.
